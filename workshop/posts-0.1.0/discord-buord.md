## R.A.T.S. Clean Screen is out
A tiny mod that switches off the beta branch's version text, which lay over window title bars:
- Hides the **version label** the beta branch draws over everything
- One setting to bring it back

**Setup** Switch it on in the Mods menu and restart.
Needs **R.A.T.S. Keel**.
**Workshop:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3836386468
**Source (MIT):** <https://github.com/struckl/SeaPowerCleanScreen>
